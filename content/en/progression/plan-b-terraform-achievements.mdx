## Early-Game Milestones: Establishing Logistics

Your first set of milestones revolves around establishing sustainable extractors, refining raw minerals, and feeding your initial colony. Early efficiency prevents resource starvations that could halt population growth later.

To secure these foundation trophies, prioritize setting up iron, sulfur, and concrete extraction lines immediately. While early trucks get cargo moving quickly, their efficiency drops sharply beyond a distance of ten hexes.

Players often observe that sudden temperature spikes can flood poorly situated low-elevation factories. Checking planetary elevation maps prior to deploying major industrial hubs protects your hard-earned progress from accidental submersion.

### Eliminating Transit Bottlenecks

1. **Avoid Single-Track Chokepoints**: Construct dedicated two-way parallel lines for high-traffic corridors connecting major mining sites to city clusters.
2. **Buffer Inventory with Large Depots**: Place intermediate storage buffers at train terminals so loading queues never idle production factories.
3. **Segregate Cargo Lines**: Isolate bulk construction materials (steel, concrete) from delicate city consumables (water, food) to keep life-support supplies flowing freely.

## Strategic Tips for 100% Completion

Earning every trophy in a single run requires disciplined long-range planning. Implement these battle-tested strategies to avoid stalling your planetary progression:

- **Survey Elevation Before Building**: Never build primary manufacturing hubs in low-altitude depressions that turn into lakebeds once the ice melts.
- **Overproduce Basic Building Materials**: Steel and concrete demands skyrocket during late-game rail expansions. Stockpile these materials early.
- **Decentralize Your Processing**: Rather than routing every raw material to a single super-factory, create specialized regional manufacturing rings.
- **Monitor Global Balances Regularly**: Keep track of atmospheric levels. Overheating the planet can trigger ecological penalties that stall your biome growth goals.
- **Backup Key Save States**: Before initiating massive planetary warming, save your game to protect your infrastructure against unexpected flooding.

With patience, optimized transit loops, and coordinated supply lines, checking off all **Plan B Terraform achievements** becomes an exceptionally rewarding milestone in your simulation gaming journey.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I earn Plan B Terraform achievements on custom or modified map seeds?
Yes, achievements generally remain obtainable on standard custom seeds as long as base gameplay parameters and difficulty scaling rules stay within standard simulation limits. Modifying internal values via third-party tools or disabling key environmental constraints may disable achievement tracking for that save file.

### What causes population growth to stall during the late game?
Population growth stalls almost exclusively due to resource delivery bottlenecks. If a city runs out of water, food, or consumer goods for even brief intervals, population expansion pauses immediately. Inspect your depot delivery frequencies and rail throughput to ensure zero downtime on life-support items.

### How do I prevent rising oceans from destroying my rail network?
Before triggering mass warming via greenhouse gas injectors, switch to the topographical elevation view. Build critical rail lines, depots, and manufacturing centers along high-elevation plateaus and ridges, leaving low-lying basins open for dynamic ocean and lake formation.

### Which Plan B Terraform achievements are considered the most difficult?
The community generally considers late-game population milestones (such as hitting sustained mega-city populations above 100,000 residents) and complete biosphere greening to be the most challenging. These milestones require sprawling, multi-continental rail grids operating at peak efficiency without material starvation.
